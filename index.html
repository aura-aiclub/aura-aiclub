ABOUT AURA

Exploring the AI frontier.

AURA is being established as a space where students can explore Artificial Intelligence through learning, experimentation, projects, events, and collaboration.

01

Learn

Create opportunities for students to learn about Artificial Intelligence, emerging technologies, tools, and real-world applications.

02

Explore

Explore new developments in AI and encourage curiosity through discussions, workshops, events, and technology-focused activities.

03

Build

Encourage students to turn ideas into practical AI projects and solutions through experimentation and collaboration.

OPERATIONS

What AURA does.

AURA will operate through a combination of learning, knowledge sharing, technical exploration, and community activities.

01

AI Knowledge

Share useful information about Artificial Intelligence, emerging technologies, research, tools, and important developments.

02

Learning & Discussion

Encourage discussions, knowledge-sharing sessions, technical conversations, and student-led learning.

03

Community

Build a community where students interested in AI can connect, exchange ideas, and work together.
